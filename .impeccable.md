# Sel design context

## Design Context

### Users
Small sales teams reviewing recorded calls, checking claims against their business documents, practicing conversations, and preparing follow-ups. Audience and jobs are recorded in README.md.

### Brand Personality
Calm, precise, considerate. Reduce fatigue during long transcript reviews. Evidence and human approval come before visual novelty.

### Aesthetic Direction
User direction, 16 October 2026: Apple-inspired restraint, light grey canvas, rounded white cards, dark-on-dark gradient buttons, soft semantic colors and smooth interactions. Preserve local Switzer fonts, Solar icons and the Sel waveform identity. Light theme only.

### Design Principles
- Keep content readable and the next action obvious.
- White surfaces sit above a quiet grey canvas; elevation expresses interactivity.
- Color always explains a state; pair it with a label or icon.
- Motion confirms input with short, settled transitions and honors reduced motion.
- Reuse the shared CSS primitives. Keep review, approval and simulator behavior intact.

See DESIGN.md and /static/design-system.html for the maintained system.
