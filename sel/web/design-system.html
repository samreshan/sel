06

Reassuring, never distracting.

Pair soft colors with explicit language. Loading, errors, and completion are part of the design—not afterthoughts.

Status vocabulary

SupportedBacked by a business source
ContradictedThe source disagrees
AmbiguousMore information is needed
Ready for approvalA person decides what happens next
DismissedRecoverable, never erased

Feedback in context

You are in control.
Sel never sends an email or creates an event for you.

When should a dialog be used?

Use a dialog for a focused decision that needs context. Keep simple edits and validation inline. Escape closes a dialog and focus returns to its trigger.

09

Felt, more than seen.

Fast feedback and a gentle settle. No bounce, no decorative loops, no movement that gets between someone and their work.

An interaction, in three beats

280 ms · gentle settle
120 msPress
180 msHover & focus
280 msReveal

Consideration is a feature.

  • Keyboard is a first-class input.

    Visible focus, native controls, logical order, and arrow keys inside tab groups.

  • Meaning goes beyond color.

    Readable text, explicit status labels, and independent evidence and playback states.

  • Comfort follows the person.

    Reduced motion, responsive layouts, browser zoom, and readable text at every size.
