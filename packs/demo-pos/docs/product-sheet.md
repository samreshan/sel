# Thali POS product sheet (fictional, for testing)

## Plans
- Standard: NPR 2,500 per month per outlet, billed yearly. Up to 3 billing counters.
- Plus: NPR 4,000 per month per outlet, billed yearly. Unlimited counters, kitchen order tickets.

A 14-day free trial is available on both plans.

## Offline mode
Billing keeps working offline and syncs when the connection returns.

## Integrations
Thali POS does not currently integrate with accounting software. Sales can be
exported as CSV at any time.
